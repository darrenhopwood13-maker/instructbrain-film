1 · Today, with the problem marked

What is wrong on the live page right now

  • The squashed block. The hero is a 1280px grid, but everything under it — the three buttons, the slogan, the report panel — is wrapped in max-w-4xl (896px) and aligned left. That leaves a 384px dead strip down the right-hand side, which is the "squashed to one side" you are seeing.
  • The buttons fight their own contents. Each button carries a label, an arrow and a 3D object, so the arrow lands mid-sentence ("Create custom report→" then the hard hat) and the third one is squeezed with whitespace-nowrap.
  • The white slab. The report mock is a full white card sitting beside the copy — it reads as a picture of a screen rather than the product's own surface.

Why this one. The screen is the pitch, so it should be the biggest object on the page instead of a prop beside the copy.
