The centrepiece — one house style

At 0:31 the film drops a different report template onto a growing pile, one after another. Eight templates, eight different documents — and every one of them carries the same brand, the same structure and the same feel: same header, same wordmark, same reference block, same footer, same green rule. That is the point. Many reports, many authors, one source of truth — so any reader knows at a glance which house the document came from, without any one client's own branding being applied to it.

Every reference shown is the Demonstration account's own. No client name and no client branding appears anywhere in the film.

What is real

  • Every product screen is a real capture of instructbrain.com at 2x, taken while signed in to the Demonstration account.
  • Every photograph is a real weatherproofing investigation photograph. Originals kept; metadata stripped; no faces.
  • Every claim is the product's own copy, or its own terms, quoted verbatim.
  • The report covers are generated for this film from the product's own twelve templates — no client documents were photographed.

What is synthetic

  • Narration — machine voice (edge-tts, en-GB-Ryan, one voice throughout).
  • Music — “Tech Background” by caboose3146, Freesound sound 345010, released CC0 1.0 (public domain) and found through the Openverse audio API. No attribution is required; it is credited anyway. Nothing was paid.
  • Captions, cards and the report-stack animation — built for the film. Not screenshots.
  • No AI footage. Every frame is a real screen, a real photograph, or type set for this film.

About the numbers on the dashboard

The dashboard shown is the Demonstration account, and its figures were deliberately dressed for this film so the account reads like a job in progress. Those figures are sample data in a demonstration account only. No live client data was changed to make this film.
