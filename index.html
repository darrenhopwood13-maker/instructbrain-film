The centrepiece — one house style

At 0:31 the film drops one real report onto a growing pile, one after another, while a list beside it ticks each one off. Every page in that pile is page one of an actual report this product generated — pulled from the app's own PDF output, not drawn. The narration names each one out loud as it lands — fire register, fit-out quality, mechanical and HVAC, damp and mould, site condition, snagging, electrical condition, weatherproofing — so the beat runs at the pace of the voice. All eight names are on screen throughout, ticking as they land. Eight real documents:

  • FC-2026-001 — Weekly compliance register, fire
  • DEMO-FQ-001 — Fit-out & finishes quality
  • DEMO-MS-001 — Mechanical & HVAC installation
  • DEMO-DM-001 — Damp, mould & water ingress
  • DEMO-SC-001 — Site condition
  • DEMO-SN-001 — Snag identification & remedial schedule
  • DEMO-EL-001 — Electrical installation condition
  • DEMO-WP-002 — Weatherproofing membrane survey

Every page carries the same house style: the same navy header band, the same wordmark, the same title position, the same reference and client block, the same green summary panel, the same footer. Different document, identical house. That is the point — many reports, many authors, one source of truth, so any reader knows at a glance which house a document came from, without any client's own branding being applied to it.

Every reference on screen is the Demonstration account's own. No client name and no client branding appears anywhere in the film.

The opening

Two real photographs, cross-faded: the building itself first — brick, zinc roof, glazing, so anyone at all can tell what they are looking at — then the failed upstand, the detail the survey found. The caption “Water where it should not be.” runs across both.

The close

The wordmark is set in Audiowide — the same face the site itself declares for its wordmark — with “instruct” in white and “Brain” in laser green. The line underneath is the site's own closing sentence, word for word: “Your next report can be off your desk before the drive home.” No pricing, no trial terms and no card wording appears in the film.

What is real

  • Every product screen is a real capture of instructbrain.com at 2x, taken while signed in to the Demonstration account.
  • Every photograph is a real weatherproofing investigation photograph. Originals kept; metadata stripped; no faces.
  • Every claim is the product's own copy, or its own terms, quoted verbatim.
  • The report pages in the pile are page one of eleven real reports this product generated, taken from its own PDF output. No client documents were used.

What is synthetic

  • Narration — machine voice (edge-tts, en-GB-Ryan, one voice throughout).
  • Music — “220Vpop-AR13-2_49” by AngeloRizzo, Freesound sound 664813, released CC0 1.0 (public domain), found via the Openverse audio API. No attribution is required; it is credited anyway. Nothing was paid.
  • Captions and cards — built for the film. The report pages they sit beside are real documents.
  • No AI footage. Every frame is a real screen, a real photograph, or type set for this film.

About the numbers on the dashboard

The dashboard shown is the Demonstration account, and its figures were deliberately dressed for this film so the account reads like a job in progress. Those figures are sample data in a demonstration account only. No live client data was changed to make this film.
