instructBrain

Website film — second cut

2 minutes 34 seconds, 18 scenes. Built from live product screens and real site photographs. Nothing on screen is invented, and there is no AI footage.

Download the master (MP4) Open the file on its own

What is real

  • Every product screen is a real capture of instructbrain.com at 2x, taken while signed in to the Demonstration account.
  • Every photograph is a real weatherproofing investigation photograph. Originals kept; metadata stripped; no faces.
  • Every claim is the product's own copy, or its own terms, quoted verbatim — including the twelve report templates and the language feature.

What is synthetic

  • Narration — machine voice (edge-tts, en-GB-Ryan), one voice throughout.
  • Music — “Tech Background” by caboose3146, Freesound sound 345010, released CC0 1.0 (public domain) and found through the Openverse audio API. No attribution is required by the licence; it is credited here anyway. Nothing was paid.
  • Captions and cards — typography rendered for the film, not screenshots.
  • No AI footage. Every frame is a real screen or a real photograph.

About the numbers on the dashboard

The dashboard shown is the Demonstration account, and its figures were deliberately dressed for this film so the account reads like a job in progress. Those figures are sample data in a demonstration account only. No live client data was changed to make this film.
